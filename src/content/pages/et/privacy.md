## Kes vastutab

Eesti Ilm is developed and maintained by Andrius Shiaulis, an independent app developer based in Estonia. The developer is responsible for the Eesti Ilm app, its widgets and this website, eestiilm.app.

Privaatsusega seotud küsimuste korral kirjuta aadressile [support@eestiilm.app](mailto:support@eestiilm.app).

## Andmed, mida rakendus kasutab

Eesti Ilm does not collect personal data. The app has:

- kontosid ega sisselogimist
- jälgimist teistes rakendustes ega veebisaitidel
- reklaame
- kolmandate osapoolte analüütika- ega reklaamikoodi

The app keeps a few details on your iPhone so that it works as expected, such as the app version shown in iOS Settings and when the app last asked you to rate it. They stay on your iPhone and are deleted when you delete the app.

## Ilmaprognoosi päringud

To show the forecast, the app and its widgets request it directly from [ilmateenistus.ee](https://www.ilmateenistus.ee/), the weather service of the Estonian Environment Agency (Keskkonnaagentuur). The request asks for the forecast for Estonia in English or Estonian. It does not include your location, your name or an identifier for your device.

Like any website, ilmateenistus.ee receives standard request data, such as your IP address and the time of the request. The agency handles this data under its own privacy policy. Eesti Ilm is an independent app and is not affiliated with the agency.

Arendajale ei saadeta midagi: rakendusel ei ole oma serverit.

## Analüütika ja kokkujooksmised

Apple sends the developer crash reports and usage statistics, such as how many devices use the app, only if you choose to share your iPhone analytics. Apple makes this data anonymous before the developer sees it, and the developer uses it only to find and fix problems in the app.

You decide whether to share it. On your iPhone, go to Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements. In the EU, one switch, Share iPhone Analytics (Share iPhone & Watch Analytics with a paired Apple Watch), covers sharing with both Apple and app developers; in some other regions, there is also a separate Share With App Developers switch.

## Asukoht

The app does not use your location. It never asks for access to it.

## Lapsed

Eesti Ilm ei kogu kelleltki isikuandmeid, ka mitte lastelt.

## See veebisait

eestiilm.app has no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. It loads its fonts and images from its own server and makes no requests to third parties.

The website is hosted by Hostinger. Like most web servers, Hostinger's servers keep standard logs of each request, such as the IP address, the page that was opened, the browser and the time. The logs are used only to run the website and keep it secure.

## Sinu õigused

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to ask what personal data is held about you, and to have it corrected or deleted. You can also object to how it is used or ask for its use to be restricted.

Because the app collects no personal data, the developer usually holds none about you. If you write to the developer, your email address and message are used only to answer you.

To use your rights, write to [support@eestiilm.app](mailto:support@eestiilm.app). You can also complain to the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate ([aki.ee](https://www.aki.ee/)) or to the data protection authority in your country.
